What moved, and why

Tokens

One design system

Colour, type scale, spacing, shadows and easing now come from the branding page's variables rather than a second set defined here.

Shape

Corners squared to 2px

The brand commits to a 2px radius. Panels, badges and buttons were between 4px and 12px, which read as a different product.

Keyboard

Reachable without a mouse

Arrow keys move along the bar and down a panel, Escape closes and returns focus to its trigger, and the drawer holds focus while it is open.

Sightline

Triggers fill the bar

Each trigger is the full 88px of nav height, so the pointer can no longer fall through a gap between a label and the panel it opened.

Position

The nav sticks

It stays at the top of the viewport and takes a shadow once the page has scrolled, instead of leaving with the first band.

Motion

Honours the system setting

Panel transitions, the hero washes and the button lift all stand down when the reader has asked for reduced motion.
